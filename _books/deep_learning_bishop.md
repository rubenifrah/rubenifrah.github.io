This book provides a comprehensive introduction to the core ideas underpinning deep learning, designed for both newcomers and experienced practitioners. 



Published in 2023, it covers modern concepts of deep learning and techniques expected to have lasting relevance. Complex ideas are presented clearly with a focus on practical applications through text, diagrams, mathematical formulas, and pseudo-code.
