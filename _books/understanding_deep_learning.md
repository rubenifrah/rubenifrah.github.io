A highly practical and visual guide to deep learning. This book excels at bridging the gap between mathematical theory and engineering practice, with nearly every concept accompanied by intuitive illustrations. It covers the landscape of modern deep learning, including very recent topics such as transformers or diffusion models.
I highly recommend this book for anyone looking to understand the inner workings of deep learning models, as a follow-up to a course in machine learning.
